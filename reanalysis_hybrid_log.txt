# Hybrid reanalysis — manuscript configuration (primary) with sensitivity analyses

Generated by `reanalysis_hybrid.py` (seed = 42 everywhere). Data loading (timestamps rounded to the hour), the 60/20/20 split, the monthly folds, metrics, DM-HAC and G14 come from `reanalysis_common.py`, shared with `reanalysis_r2.py`. All numbers have 3 decimals. Residual e = Y − Ŷ (positive = actual above baseline). CV(RMSE) and NMBE in %.

**Primary configuration (manuscript, no tuning):** one-hot Hour (ref 00) + one-hot DoW (ref Monday) + Lag-1 + Lag-24; LR = OLS; RF = scikit-learn defaults with n_estimators = 100, random_state = 42. LR vs RF is decided by validation RMSE only; the chosen model is refitted on Train+Validation and scored once on Test. Benchmarks: constant mean (mean of the fitting window, i.e. Train for validation and Train+Validation for test) and persistence Ŷ(t) = Y(t−1).

*Note on column order.* The one-hot columns are kept in the order of the round-1 script (`pd.get_dummies` on string labels: hour_1, hour_10 … hour_19, hour_2, hour_20 … hour_9, dow_1 … dow_6). With `max_features = 1.0` the RF breaks ties between equally good splits by column position, so this order is needed to reproduce the manuscript to the last digit; the encoding itself is identical. Sensitivity (d) shows the size of this effect.

## 10. Deviation analysis on the untouched test set (selected model: RF)

PRF = share of hours with a positive residual (Y > Ŷ; corrected Eq. 9, a sign statistic, not waste). E⁺ = Σ max(Y − Ŷ, 0) kWh, E⁻ = Σ max(Ŷ − Y, 0) kWh, net = E⁺ − E⁻ (Δt = 1 h). τ = 2 × the model's validation RMSE (fixed before test). 'Σ e over hours > τ' is the round-1 definition (whole residual of the flagged hours); 'Σ (e − τ)' counts only the part above τ. The LR row is shown for comparison.
